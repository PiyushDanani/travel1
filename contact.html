Frequently Asked Questions

HAVE QUESTIONS? WE ARE HERE TO HELP

Common queries from our clients in Lira City regarding our booking process and services.

How do I apply for USA, Schengen, Australia, or UK Visas with Travelicious?

Travelicious specializes in USA (B1/B2, F1), Schengen (Europe 29 countries), Australia (Subclass 600), and UK visitor visas. Director Irfan Sheikh and our team manage your portal application (DS-160, ImmiAccount, Access UK), review your financials and ties to Uganda, schedule your VFS/Embassy appointment in Kampala, and conduct mock interview prep.

How do I apply for a tourist or visit visa through Travelicious?

Simply visit our office on Obote Avenue Main Street in Lira City or message Director Irfan Sheikh on WhatsApp (+256 751 510 786). We review your passport, provide the exact checklist, format all required documents, and guide you through submission.

Can you customize a holiday package to fit my specific budget?

Yes, absolutely! Whether you want a luxury 5-star Dubai getaway or a cost-effective family vacation to Zanzibar or Europe, we tailor flight schedules, hotel star-ratings, and daily tours to match your budget.

Do you provide flight tickets for both domestic and international airlines?

Yes, we issue verified e-tickets for all domestic routes in Uganda and East Africa, as well as all premier international carriers worldwide with competitive fares and transparent change/cancellation policies.

How do I confirm my booking from outside Lira City?

You can easily complete your booking remotely. We accept consultations and payments through authorized bank transfer and mobile money, and send your flight e-tickets, hotel vouchers, and visa confirmations directly to your WhatsApp and email.
